Attention Half-Life

A day of global attention only changes you if nobody knew you before.

Wikipedia publishes how many people read every page, every day. Below is every occasion since 2015 on which the world stopped and read the same thing at once, and what was left of it a year later.

Start with one of them

The shape is that page's real daily readership, from two weeks before through two months after. The dotted line is what the page got on an ordinary day.

Every event, each scaled to its own biggest day so the shapes lie on top of one another. Hover any thread.

One dot per event, placed on the day its page was back to being read at close to its own ordinary rate. The ones that never got there are gathered at the end.

What decides whether it sticks

Not how big the day was. How many people were reading that page beforehand. Each dot drops to the line it started from, so the length of the drop is what the event actually changed.
